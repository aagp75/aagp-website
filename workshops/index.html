AAGP Workshop 2025

The AAGP Education Committee is thrilled to announce our upcoming in-person 2025 Conference "AI in GIS". With the growing prominence of Artificial Intelligence, this theme is pertinent as we look back on 50 years as an association, and move forward as an industry. GeoAI like any form of AI is a powerful combination that has the potential to change how we operate and there are many technical and ethical challenges that need to be addressed as this new chapter in GIS and Planning emerges. What will be be doing in 5 to 10 years, who will be doing it, and will it be for the good or bad?



Date: Thursday/Friday June 12th and 13th

Location: NSCC Ivany Campus (Waterfront), 81 Mawiomi Pl, Dartmouth

Fee: $200 for members* ; $325 for non-members (or $75 membership fee for those eligible* + $200 conference fee = $275)

*New members, please see our "Join the AAGP" page for membership application details.



Sneak Peek at the Agenda

  • Kelsey Green (Eagle Engineering and Consulting)
  • Yan Chen (Dalhousie University) - AI Powered Spatial Analysis: Urban Tree Canopy Detection
  • Robert Newcombe (AI-First Consulting) - Integrating AI: Workflows, Strategies, and Best Practices
  • Amin Zribi (Digital Nova Scotia) - Emerging Wireless Technologies, IoT, and the Future of Smart Cities: A GeoAI Perspective
  • Ashley Wu and Jeff Caddell (Property Valuation Services Corporation) - How PVSC Revolutionized Valuation with AI

Don't miss this opportunity to engage with industry leaders, academics, and fellow professionals passionate about shaping the future of our communities

About Our Workshops

Our workshops typically take place in June at the Centre of Geographic Sciences (NSCC COGS) in Lawrencetown (Annapolis Valley), Nova Scotia. Occasionally, workshops are held in other locations within the Atlantic Provinces depending upon membership distribution and facility availability.

Some of the topics covered at past workshops include:

  • GeoAI
  • Resiliency Rising: Spatial Strategies for Stronger, Safer Communities
  • Integrating CAD Data with ArcGIS
  • Leveraging ArcGIS Solutions for Asset Management
  • Field Mapping Technologies
  • Supervised Land Use Classification
  • Working with LiDAR Data
  • What's New in ArcGIS & ArcMap Editing Tips and Tricks
  • ArcGIS Tools for Evaluating and Managing Watersheds
  • ArcGIS Viewshed Analysis
  • ArcGIS VBA Customization and Applications
  • Working with NS Community Counts Data
  • SketchUp Training (Basic and Advanced)
  • Statistics Canada GeoSuite Data and ArcGIS
  • Digital Elevation Modeling
  • River Flood Gradient Modelling
  • Storm Surge Flood Modelling
  • Municipal Water Supply Watershed Planning
  • Establishing and Maintaining Riparian Forest Buffers
  • Desktop Security Awareness
  • EMO - Emergency Scenario Training
  • Tours - NS EMO/EOC, NS Registry of Deeds, NS Geomatics Centre

AAGP workshops are sponsored by the Nova Scotia Department of Municipal Affairs and organized and operated with the assistance of the NSCC COGS.
