Get Involved

The following page provides information on the qualifications for membership into the association. Should you wish to apply for membership in the Association you can complete and submit an online membership application.

Some of the benefits of membership may include:

  • Access to specialized training
  • Discounted workshop fees and priority seating at workshops
  • Discounts on software and external training
  • Access to our to network with fellow members, academia, and industry collegues
  • Contribution and access to newsletters
  • Access to social events

Range of typical job titles holding membership

  • Planning Technician / Technologist / Officer
  • Development Technician / Officer
  • Director of Planning
  • GIS Analyst / Officer / Specialist / Technician / Technologist
  • Urban Design Technician
  • Geomatics Consultant
  • Engineering Technologist
  • Transportation / Civil Technician
  • Civic Addressing Technician / Coordinator

Join the AAGP

We offer an array of memberships levels to fit your current and future career status. The information below will assist in determining if you qualify to apply for membership.




FULL MEMBERSHIP: $75 annually *

ASSOCIATE MEMBERSHIP: $50 annually *

STUDENT MEMBERSHIP: $10 annually

RETIRED MEMBERSHIP: $10 annually

Note: Student and Associate members have the same rights and privileges as that of Full members with the exceptions being that they cannot vote on any matter and cannot be an officer or councillor of the association.

* Organizations who have five (5) or more members enrolled in the AAGP are eligible to receive a 20% discount on their membership fees (group membership rate commenced in June 2023)

FULL MEMBERSHIP may be granted at the discretion of the Membership Committee to a resident who has applied to be a member who:

  • Graduated from a program related to Geomatics or Planning of an accredited post-secondary institution with more than one (1) year of work experience in the Geomatics or Planning fields.

OR

  • Direct experience working in Geomatics or Planning for four (4) years with no formal education in the field.

ASSOCIATE MEMBERSHIP may be granted at the discretion of the Membership Committee to an individual who has applied to be a member and who has:

  • Graduated from a program related to Geomatics or Planning of an accredited post-secondary institution, with less than one (1) year of full-time work experience in the Geomatics or Planning fields.

OR

  • Direct experience working in Geomatics or Planning of less than four (4) years with no formal education in the field.

STUDENT MEMBERSHIP may be granted at the discretion of the Membership Committee to an individual who is enrolled in a program and / or course related to Geomatics or Planning from an accredited post-secondary institution.


RETIRED MEMBERSHIP may be granted at the discretion of the Membership Committee to an individual who has retired from working in Geomatics and Planning.


We accept the following forms of payment: cash, cheque, money order, e-transfer.
