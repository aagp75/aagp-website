Spotlight

Major AI Conference Coming to Halifax May 2026

Join tech leaders from across Canada and from Europe for AICON Halifax – a groundbreaking international forum exploring the social impact of artificial intelligence:

https://www.ctvnews.ca/atlantic/nova-scotia/article/major-ai-conference-coming-to-halifax-next-spring/

Building on AICON conferences led by Belfast-based tech powerhouse Kainos in both Belfast and Silicon Valley, this inaugural Halifax edition brings together leading voices from business, academia, and government to ask: How can AI be harnessed to transform public services while building stronger, more prosperous and more inclusive communities?

Our People-First approach to NI will be distinctively Nova Scotian but include voices from Ireland, the UK and from across Canada.

Belfast and Halifax share deep-rooted connections through history, migration, and culture.

Now, AICON Halifax aims to reimagine those ties for the digital age – forging a new chapter of transatlantic innovation and collaboration and shining a spotlight on the tech advances of both cities.

May 28, 2026 - Halifax, Nova Scotia.
