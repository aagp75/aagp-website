Workshop 2025: GeoAI

With the growing prominence of Artificial Intelligence, the 2025 conference theme was AI in GIS. Given the 50th anniversary of the association, this was a pertinent theme as we look to move forward as an association and as an industry. GeoAI and Location Intelligence like any form of AI is a powerful combination that has the potential to change how we operate and there are many technical and ethical challenges that need to be addressed as this new chapter in GIS and Planning emerges. What will we be doing in 5 to 10 years, who will be doing it, and will it be for the good or bad?

LAStools - Efficient Tools for LiDAR Processing

Date: September 24, 2012

Date: February 15, 2012

Presenter: Dr. Martin Isenburg

Venue: Halifax Regonal Municipality

Dr. Isenburg will present a talk on the technology in LAStools and how it can be used for efficient LiDAR processing. He will discuss a project in the Canary Islands (Spain) where LAStools were used to create canopy height maps, forest density grids, and elevation rasters. The processing results also helped to improve the existing photogrammetrically derived topographic maps significantly. The vegetation-penetrating lasers uncovered elevation differences of up to 25 meters between the official records and reality where entire hills and valleys had been hiding under the canopy of the dense local pine forest.

LiDAR Bathymetry - Technical Overview

Presentation 2012

Date: February 15, 2012

Presenter: Michael Broadbent, Fugro

Venue: Bedford Institute of Oceanography

The presentation will consist of an in depth look at data collection, processing and derived products from bathymetic lidar system including seabed reflectance. Advances in processing procedures to allow bathymetric surveys to be conducted without the requirement of a tide gauge will be outlined. The processing from laser waveforms to discrete point clouds will be discussed and the research into the potential to obtain water column characteristics from the waveform will be discussed. Analysis of the point cloud and derived products such as the variance of the returns to characterize the sea bed and gridding routines for the production of bathymetric grids will be presented. A look at where the technology and industry is going in the future as new applications of these data are explored will also be discussed.
